## 🎓 **HOW IT WORKS:**

### **Course Detail Page URL Pattern:**
```
http://localhost:8080/course-detail.html?course=<course-id>
```

### **Examples:**
- **PL/SQL:** `course-detail.html?course=oracle-plsql`
- **ChatGPT:** `course-detail.html?course=chatgpt-mastery`
- **PostgreSQL:** `course-detail.html?course=postgresql-complete`
- **Vector DBs:** `course-detail.html?course=vector-databases-ai`

### **What Gets Generated:**
For each course, the page auto-generates:
1. ✅ **Course Title & Track**
2. ✅ **Rating & Student Count**
3. ✅ **Level, Duration, Mode**
4. ✅ **Price & Next Batch**
5. ✅ **Description**
6. ✅ **Learning Outcomes** (6 bullets)
7. ✅ **Prerequisites** (3 bullets)
8. ✅ **Modules** (4 modules with topics)
9. ✅ **Instructor Info**
10. ✅ **Career Opportunities**
11. ✅ **Career Growth Path**
